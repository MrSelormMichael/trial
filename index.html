Heritage

Made by hand, in Accra.

Velma's began as a millinery practice and grew into a studio for headpieces, turbans and clutches rooted in Ghanaian craft. Every piece is cut, beaded and finished in-house.

We carry the symbols we were raised with — Sankofa, Gye Nyame, Adinkrahene — not as ornament, but as reminders of the standard we hold.
